# Save slots. S on the menu shows the game's nine slots (/Save Games/<game>) as pictures: the
# chosen one large, the nine in a grid (an empty one a box with its number); O P Q A choose;
# ENTER or a number saves to one (asking before saving over one); L labels the chosen slot (an
