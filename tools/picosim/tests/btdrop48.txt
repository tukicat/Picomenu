# B - Bluetooth tape when the receiver goes away (switched off, out of range, taken by a phone):
# the Bluetooth is started afresh at once (as O, then B), the B screen says why, and C connects
# again; then the receiver goes away in the middle of a load: BREAK, and LOAD "" connects again by
# itself (the receiver paired last) and plays the tape.

sd /Tapes/tapein.tap {HERE}/tapein.tap

nmi
wait 30

key 1
wait 50
key t

key t
wait 50
nmi
wait 30
key t
wait 30
key b
wait 30
key s
wait 60
key 1
wait 30

key BREAK
wait 30
key x
wait 30
key x
wait 30

btdrop
wait 50
nmi
wait 30
key t
wait 30
key b
wait 30

expect Paired: Speccy BT receiver
expect C-Connect
key c
wait 50
expect On Speccy BT receiver
key BREAK
wait 30
key x
wait 30
key x
wait 30
# gone in the middle of a load (the header's tone): BREAK, then LOAD "" again
key j

key ENTER

wait 50
