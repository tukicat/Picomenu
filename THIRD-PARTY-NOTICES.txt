third-party code was added for Rev 2.0 to Rev 2.3.0; Rev 2.4.0 adds the Pico W build

is gone.

The Spectrum ROM images and any games or snapshots you use with the
Picoface are not covered by any licence here. None are in this source: the
tape converter reads 48.rom and 128.rom (or 128-0.rom and 128-1.rom) that you
put on the card or flash yourself, in its root or in /ROMS. The firmware only
holds a few bytes that match the 48K ROM, to check it before changing it in
memory (the start of LD-BYTES and of the start up) and to put back the 8 bytes of
its (c) character that the tape hook's mailbox borrows. Some simulator scripts
name commercial tapes (Out Run, Gauntlet, Athena ...): they are not included,
and the scripts look for them in a folder you give.
